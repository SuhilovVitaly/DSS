## Implementation steps

1. AcceptPassenger проверяет docked origin, активный offer/revision, count<=freeCabins и жизнь носителя; резервирует реальных людей без изменения CrewId экипажа и без списания выдуманного залога.
2. A-01: срок ordinary = ceil(authoritative ETA × 2), urgent = ceil(ETA × 1.25) от GameTimeMs принятия, минимум один календарный час; коэффициенты content. ETA захватывается, deadline не продлевается после Load.
3. При подтверждённой стыковке нужной destination и arrivalTime<=deadline высадить только пассажиров contractId, один раз начислить payout и RecordVoyageAmount с durable postingId. Другие станции/повтор arrival денег не дают.
4. Включить passenger count в фактическое питание/life support; состояние Active/Completed, deadlines и receipt сохранять до показанного успеха.
5. Сохранить state, identity counters и receipts в соответствующем typed разделе DemoCampaignData; зарегистрировать handler/tick/projection через hooks EP-0032-US-0007. Duplicate commit и save/load не повторяют расходы, выплаты или события.

Assumptions:

- A-01 (ticket): deadline basis stays the US-0006 offer rule (captured at acceptance, never extended on Load): `max(minimum, ceil(ETA × permille / 1000))` where ETA is the authoritative route estimate (`routeKm / maxSpeed`, calendar ×300).
- A-04 (technical, replaces the ×2 / ×1.25 baseline of A-01): a command-driven flight on the shortest demo route (15 km) takes ≈1.2 h of calendar time with a three-burn pilot and ≈8.8 h at maximum speed (turn radius), while the max-speed ETA is 12.5 min, so ×2 / ×1.25 (and the 1 h minimum) made every delivery late. Content multipliers are now ordinary 12000‰, urgent 8000‰ (ratio 1.5 kept; ordinary 2.5 h / urgent 1.67 h on the shortest route). Cargo (2500‰) has the same issue and is left to US-0004, which owns cargo delivery. Balance remains a playtest baseline.
- A-05: delivery requires a confirmed docking (dialogue finished); it runs in the snapshot/save tick, also while paused, and is idempotent through the `Completed` status and the receipt. Arrival after the deadline pays nothing and keeps the passengers aboard as `overdue`; failure, penalty and cancellation belong to US-0003.
- A-06: the payout is posted to the voyage ledger only when the latest ledger is the arrival at this station (docking without a voyage still pays, without a posting), so a payout is never attached to an unrelated voyage.
- A-07: no save-format bump — `passengerContracts` is a new optional section of the schema-1 campaign data (A-P2 not triggered). Legacy US-0006 active passenger obligations without boarded passengers keep their promised seats and close once on delivery with an empty passenger list.
- A-08: passenger display names equal their stable ids (named passengers/dialogue are non-goals of the story).
